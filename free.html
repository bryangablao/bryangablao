cmd.bat | Your Secret Music Player

Looks like a command prompt. Works like a music player. 🤫

Works on PC & laptop only Windows desktop / laptop required
It looks like you're not on a Windows PC or laptop. This app (.bat) won't run on phones, tablets, Mac, or Linux.
preview

Enjoy your favorite music with a discreet, command-line interface that lets you play songs, pause, skip tracks, adjust volume, and check what’s playing, all through simple commands.

No flashy music app. No obvious interface. Just a good old CMD window doing more than anyone would expect. 👀

Your music. Your commands. Your little secret.

How it works: download cmd.bat, run it on your Windows PC or laptop, then just type a song name. Anything that isn’t a command is searched as a song.

Stealth mode: turn it on with stealth on and the window shows a fake “not recognized” message after you type a song, so it looks like a normal typo.

Need the full command list? Press the ? button above or use View all commands below.

  • Play & pause music
  • Skip tracks
  • Adjust volume
  • Check what’s playing
  • Search any song
  • Stealth mode
